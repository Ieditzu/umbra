The paper's 3–5 page requirement, documentation's five-page maximum and monochrome
document printing come from the instructions supplied by the team/coordinator.

The poster-size rule is verified against the organizer's
[RoSEF 2026 regulation](https://cygnus.org.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Regulament-RoSEF-2026.pdf),
document no. 1119 / 29.12.2025, Article 6(4), section II, PDF page 4. It sets the
maximum poster size at 80 × 120 cm and requires vertical display.

Print the current poster at **100% / actual size**, with custom paper size
800 × 1200 mm. A0 is 841 × 1189 mm and exceeds the width limit. The older local
`umbra-poster-a0-canva.pdf` and earlier Canva designs are superseded; do not send
them to the printer. The current editable Canva link is recorded in the
[poster instructions](poster/scientific/README.md).

The three schools appear directly beneath their respective authors at the top
of the current poster. Its text and vector graph components remain editable.

All documentation pages were rendered and reviewed. Page counts and PDF page
boxes were checked; the paper and documentation render in grayscale. This check
does not constitute a physical print proof from the print shop.
